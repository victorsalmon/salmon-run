# Client Onboarding Checklist

> Fill out all **Required** fields before onboarding begins.
> **Optional** items can be deferred but may delay full setup.

## Legal Entity

| # | Item | Required | Notes |
|---|------|----------|-------|
| 1 | Full legal name (as registered) | Required | Match business registry exactly |
| 2 | Jurisdiction of incorporation | Required | Province/state and country |
| 3 | Business number / Tax ID | Required | CRA BN, IRS EIN, or equivalent |
| 4 | GST/HST status | Required | Registered? Filing frequency? |
| 5 | Fiscal year end | Required | e.g., December 31 |
| 6 | Business structure | Required | Sole prop, partnership, corp, trust |

## Bank Accounts

For each account, provide:

| # | Item | Required | Notes |
|---|------|----------|-------|
| 7 | Institution name | Required | |
| 8 | Account type | Required | Chequing / Savings / Credit Card / Line of Credit |
| 9 | Account number | Required | |
| 10 | Branch / Transit number | Required | For Canadian accounts |
| 11 | Access method | Required | Online banking / Statement PDFs / Plaid / Other |
| 12 | Signing authority on account | Required | Who has signing authority? |
| 13 | Monthly statement day | Optional | Day of month statements are generated |
| 14 | Online banking URL | Optional | |

## Email

| # | Item | Required | Notes |
|---|------|----------|-------|
| 15 | Preferred mailbox name | Required | e.g., `ap@client.ca` |
| 16 | Domain name(s) | Required | Where is email hosted? |
| 17 | Current email provider | Required | cPanel, InterWorx, Gmail, etc. |
| 18 | Forwarding rules needed | Optional | Auto-forward receipts to bookkeeping? |
| 19 | Auto-responder needs | Optional | Out-of-office, etc. |
| 20 | Existing mailbox access | Required | Can we access the existing mailbox? |
| 21 | IMAP/SMTP credentials | Required | If self-hosted email |

## Signing Authority

| # | Item | Required | Notes |
|---|------|----------|-------|
| 22 | Who can authorize account changes? | Required | Name and title |
| 23 | Who can sign tax returns? | Required | May differ from above |
| 24 | Who can authorize payments? | Optional | For bill pay setup |
| 25 | Secondary contact for approvals | Optional | Backup person |

| # | Item | Required | Notes |
