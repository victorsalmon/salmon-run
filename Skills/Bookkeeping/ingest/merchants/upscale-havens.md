# Upscale Havens — DBA Note

> **Upscale Havens** is the Doing Business As (DBA) name for **Intersite Consulting Inc.** It is not a separate entity or client.

## Context

Upscale Havens receipts are generated by me (Victor) as invoices from Intersite Consulting to my Room-Rentals business. They represent payments for property management services that Intersite Consulting provides to the rental properties — money moving from my personal rental income (Room-Rentals) to my corporation (Intersite Consulting).

## TAS Treatment

| Side | TAS | Category |
|------|-----|----------|
| Room-Rentals (payer) | room-rentals `TAS-2026.csv` | Expense (Consultant Expense or similar) |
| Intersite Consulting (payee) | intersite-consulting `TAS-2026.csv` | Consulting Revenue |

## Bank Statement Appearance

On RBC statements, Upscale Havens e-Transfers show as:

```
E-TRANSFER AUTODEPOSIT VICTOR SALMON
```

Because RBC shows the **recipient's** name, not the sender's. Cross-reference amount against known invoice patterns ($219.98, $246.23, $283.50, $690.35 are common).

## Default Deduction Category

`Consultant Expense` (Room Rentals) / `Consulting Revenue` (Intersite Consulting)
